## Rationale

The manuscript reports original computational crystallographic methodology,
numerical evidence, public diffraction-data evaluations, prospective outcomes,
and explicit failure boundaries. This aligns directly with the journal's scope
for advanced computational methods, data interpretation, numerical analysis,
and structural-research software.

A Research Paper is more appropriate than the journal's Computer Programs
category. The contribution is an empirical methods study rather than a
software announcement, and independent successful use by two users—the normal
expectation for a Computer Programs article—has not yet been established.

## Publication route

The journal has no submission, page, or colour charges. Because this work is
self-funded, the default plan is the ordinary no-fee publication route rather
than optional paid open access. If accepted, the author-accepted manuscript can
be deposited under the journal's green-open-access policy. The public software
and evidence repository remains openly available regardless of the article's
publication route.

## Submission-specific requirements already addressed

- Research Paper length is below the journal's normal 15,000-word ceiling.
- The title contains 11 words, within the journal's suggested 10–12-word
  range.
- A one-to-two-sentence synopsis is included.
- The abstract avoids first-person language and references to tables or
  figures.
- Six keywords are supplied.
- References use author–year citations and are ordered alphabetically.
- Funding, conflict-of-interest, author-contribution, data-and-code, and
  human–AI disclosure sections are present.
- The AI disclosure identifies the author as accountable and describes the
  uses of AI assistance.
- The exact `v0.1.1` release files are archived under Zenodo version DOI
  `10.5281/zenodo.21654414`, linked to concept DOI
  `10.5281/zenodo.21639283`.
- A single-column, double-spaced Word preprint has been produced from the
  published IUCr requirements.
- A 600-dpi pipeline figure is embedded in the manuscript and supplied
  separately.
- A separate 600-dpi graphical abstract is supplied.
- Both evidence tables remain in the article because they are central to the
  interpretation; no redundant supporting-information file is planned.
